# AI-drafted, cited, learner-verified Curriculum with Sealed spoilers

Claude drafts every lesson, Lab, Deliverable and question, and cites a free primary source for each claim. The Learner verifies the drafts against those citations and against an independent second source, the *CSSLP All-in-One* (3rd ed.). Every error the Learner finds becomes a rewarded Content Bug.

The first Learner is also the builder who approves the build, which would spoil the material for them. So the following are Sealed:
- solutions
- Flags
- vulnerable-code specifics
- reference answers
- the Question Bank
- the Simulation pool

CI proves each Sealed artifact works, and the Learner verifies it the first time they meet it while studying. The builder reviews only designs, Engine code, lessons and Lab Briefs.

## Considered Options
- **Co-authoring, with the Learner writing the lessons.** Rejected: it adds roughly 60–100 hours at a pace of 6–10 hours per week.
- **Unverified generation.** Rejected: AI-drafted exam content can be confidently wrong.
- **Full builder review of everything.** Rejected: it would spoil Red → Blue → Explain and the Boss Fights for the first Learner.
